# Home de NEXUS

Mode: Persuade. Scope: the whole home: navbar, hero, and the sections below (extension of the approved hero world, 2026-11-10).
Audience: litigating lawyers, insurers, investigators and expert witnesses, companies. Action: "Solicitar asesoría" via WhatsApp, with email and phone as alternatives (Aaron, 10-11: no form). Proof: Domingo in his IAAI uniform, his credentials, the method, an illustrative review report.
Constraints: Aaron's approved design (`referencias/hero-diseno.png`) is pinned and the world is not reopened. No image generation, so the build is code-led. The logo is provisional, typeset as text, until the SVG arrives. The three IAAI seals from the client's PDF are used in Perfil (Aaron, 10-11; the client confirms usage before publishing). "Formación especializada" ships as the sixth service (Aaron, 10-11), with the brochure text only. No cases, clients, or testimonials are invented. The illustrative report is labelled "Ejemplo ilustrativo, no corresponde a un caso real" (Aaron approved it, 10-11). Four photos supplied by Aaron (AI-generated, two show Domingo) are used without a caption at his request; Domingo approves them before publishing.

Aaron's standing direction (10-11): one message per section, more visual than text (photos, the X diagram, icons, the report), short lists as pill chips, two-line direct titles, bold key words, filled icons in gold circles, rounded photos to match the pill buttons. Nav: Servicios · Método · Quién soy · Contacto · Solicitar asesoría.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The fire is the setting and the investigator is the subject. The title ignites and is left charred behind him, the way a scene stays once the fire is out. This rejects the generic firefighter hero, with no flames in the foreground and no red alarm. Below the fold, the page owns "the thread": NEXUS is the knot where law, forensic science, and fire investigation meet, and every conclusion stays tied to its evidence. This refuses the consultancy template of icon cards and stat rows.
OWN-WORLD: Warm smoke-black ground (#120f0d), charred title in heavy condensed italic (grey ash cooling to dull ember), sand gold #c9a26b in pill buttons and a hairline rule, white text with wide tracking. Embers are the only saturated light. Below the fold, gold hairlines are the only connective device, and one bone-paper document is the only light surface.
STORY: In one second the visitor understands "fire investigation", sees a real expert in uniform and finds the action. They believe it is serious, not a spectacle. Scrolling, they: see the three disciplines converge into the X (Intersección), find their own situation among six services (Servicios), follow evidence to conclusion (Método), see what a review delivers (Resultado), meet the director's record (Perfil), and close on the reignited call (Contacto).
FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav on top: NEXUS on the left, the links and a gold pill on the right. A two-line title at ~88% width, centred, starting at 15% from the top. Domingo centred, taking ~85% of the height, in front of the title and dissolving at the bottom. Over his torso: the name in tracked caps, the gold rule, the subtitle, and the two CTAs side by side, the gold one first.
FORM: pinned design (Aaron), position 1, seed key: pinned-by-user (no roll; the user's approved design pins the world). The sections extend it, so there is no concept roll.
SIGNATURE: Ignition. Each letter of the title lights up in white-orange and cools to charcoal while a burst of sparks rises. Six layers with mouse and scroll parallax: photographic smoke, WebGL smoke, back embers, title, person, front embers in bokeh. Below the fold: three threads draw into the gold X; a scroll-driven gold line travels Datos → Análisis → Hipótesis → Conclusión and lights each node; review marks write themselves onto the illustrative report; the closing title reignites like the hero.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Sections and purpose

- Intersección: one block. Two-line title, one line of text, who it serves as icon chips, the X diagram, and the field photo at the end.
- Servicios: six services listed by name with an icon, the client's situation underneath. Master-detail on desktop, accordion on mobile. Each panel: one line, three chips, and a WhatsApp CTA naming the service.
- Método: one line, NFPA 921 and NFPA 1033 as chips, the evidence photo, and the scroll-driven chain (Datos, Análisis, Hipótesis, Conclusión) that lights once and stays lit.
- Resultado: one line, the three levels as chips, and the illustrative report.
- Perfil: name, role, the three IAAI seals, a one-sentence bio, the dated ledger, and the meeting photo.
- Contacto: the reignited title, one line, the WhatsApp CTA, and three channels.
